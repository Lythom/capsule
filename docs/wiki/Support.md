# Support

Most questions are answered in the [FAQ](Home#faq). To get help, either to use the Capsule mod as a player or to configure it in your modpack:

* Discord server: https://discord.gg/wZpBVdr
* GitHub issues: https://github.com/Lythom/capsule/issues

## Reporting a bug

Please include:

* the Minecraft version, the loader (NeoForge, Fabric or Forge) and its version, and the Capsule version (from the jar name, i.e. `Capsule-neoforge-1.21.1-9.0.x.jar`);
* what you did, what you expected and what happened instead; screenshots help a lot for preview or rendering issues;
* the log (`logs/latest.log`) or the crash report (`crash-reports/`), uploaded to https://mclo.gs or a gist;
* the mod list, or the modpack name and version.

If a chat message says "Capsule cannot check the claims of …: captures and deploys are refused", a claim mod changed its API: please report it with the versions of that mod and of Capsule.
